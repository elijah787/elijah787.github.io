How it works

  1. Open your Dashboard and find Create Discount Voucher.
  2. Enter the points to use and tap Generate Discount Voucher.
  3. At the till, tap Apply Voucher on your receipt, or show the voucher QR.

Points are deducted when you create the voucher. Your tier is not affected. Maximum voucher size depends on your tier, see the Tiers page.

Tutorial

How it works

A quick guide for members and for staff.

👤 Members

  1. Sign up and get your JC ID.
  2. Tap Start Shopping and show the QR to the salesperson.
  3. Your receipt appears on your phone as items are added.
  4. Optionally apply a voucher, then tap Confirm Payment.
  5. Show the confirmation QR. Your points arrive instantly.

👔 Staff

  1. Staff Login from the login page.
  2. Scan Member QR to start a sale.
  3. Add items with the quick buttons or a custom item.
  4. Send to Member and have them scan the receipt QR.
  5. Scan Member Confirmation to complete the sale.
  6. Redeem vouchers by scanning the voucher QR or typing the code.

Quick answers

Can I lose my tier?

No. Tiers use lifetime points, which never decrease.

Can I use two vouchers at once?

No, one voucher per transaction.

How long do vouchers last?

30 days from the day you create them.

I lost my voucher QR.

Open your Dashboard, go to My Discount Vouchers and tap Show QR.

My sale was interrupted.

Use the Resume Transaction button on your dashboard.
